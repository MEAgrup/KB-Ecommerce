## Deck A — "Day 2 TSP" (audiens & trafik)

**A1. Slide p37 — "First purchase & follower conversion also affect traffic"**
Flowchart: "new user" bercabang dua.
- **Content side:** non-follower / new follower / old follower. "new follower" → **content traffic** → **follow rate**.
- **E-commerce side:** not purchased / first purchase / repeat purchase. "first purchase" → **transaction traffic** → **first purchase rate**.
Inti: pembelian pertama & konversi follower ikut memengaruhi alokasi trafik, bukan cuma tontonan.

## Deck B — "Day 1 part 4 TSP" (product card matrix & industrialisasi video pendek)

**B1. Slide p40 — "Core Methodology: Vector Decomposition & Matrix Layout"**
Tinggalkan "optimasi link satu-satu", pindah ke **layout sistematis** ("single point" → "system").
Empat komponen: **Vector Definition** (4 dimensi inti: user needs, content visuals, product
attributes, competitive environment) · **Decomposition Process** (analisa kompetitor → keyword
mining → main image fission → multi-link combination launch) · **Matrix Thinking** (semua link
= satu paket aset) · **Testing Concept** ("small amount, multiple groups").

**B2. Slide p41 — "Four-Stage Matrix Path for Product Cards"**
Loop Research–Construction–Optimization–Review: **Stage 1** Research/Analysis/Fission Planning ·
**Stage 2** Construction & Execution · **Stage 3** Data Optimization (A/B testing, CTR & CVR) ·
**Stage 4** Review & Methodology Consolidation (SOP).

**B3. Slide p39 — "Product card ideas" (contoh kaus putih 99 yuan)**
Vektor melingkar: purchase intention (no scenario / daily commuting / light sports), gender,
generation, nine categories, + atribut (material, craftsmanship, combed cotton, tech texture,
anti-nipple show, sweat/wrinkle resistance, cooling, sun protection, ice texture, quick dry).
Alur: user basic attributes → purchase intention & product features → image vector elements.

**B4. Slide p43 — "Product Card Matrix Layout Example - Food" (spreadsheet Soy Sauce)**
Tabel "E-commerce Sellable Item Detail": kolom Serial#, Product Image, Product Name,
Specifications, Material Number, Is it Zero Additive, Grade, Retail Price, Lowest Price,
Product Positioning, Recent 30-Day Sales. Contoh positioning: "Brand Image Item" (sales 0),
"Core Main Push Item" (sales 55.180 / 766 / 50.737).

**B5. Slide p42 — "Product Card Matrix Layout Case - Food" (tabel eksekusi)**
Kolom: No, Phase, Task Name, Tools/Execution Standard, Delivery Date, Owner, Deliverables,
Remarks. Fase mencakup Preparation (keyword collection, SPU assortment), Copywriting Planning,
Main-image Shooting/Design, Listing Execution, Operations Details & Optimization, Practical
Review/Campaign Optimization.

**B6. Slide p44 — "Product Card Matrix Layout Case Study - Food"**
"From 'Zero Order Islands' to 'Full-Domain Linkage'": (1) LiveStreaming/Influencer Cold Start
Breakthrough — pancing penjualan awal listing baru via live/mid-tier influencer; (2) Scenario-Based
Fission & A/B Testing — banyak listing terdiferensiasi, andalkan data; (3) Organizational
Assessment Unlocks Synergy — tim short-video jadi support, nilai product card dari sales toko.

**B7. Slide p33 — "Short Video Script Copywriting Framework" (case Tea)**
Struktur naskah video: **3-Second Hook → Installation USP 1/2/3 → Usage Outcome → Guided Action
(CTA)**. Contoh Script Copy dengan hitung karakter (23/28/37/64/64/23) & Estimated Duration
(4–6S, 6–8S, 12–16S) + Storyboard Plan. Strategi video **TA×5A**; struktur B-roll (supporting) +
A-roll (host on-camera); tipe video: one-shot, template, fission/viral.
