1. **WHERE \- Enrich the LS scenarios**

   Where description: what is innovation 'where' \+ why important

   Standards of elements, detail requirements, good example (pass) \+ bad example (not pass)

   #### **Where description:**

   WHERE innovation **transforms *where* you host your livestream**, moving beyond generic studios to **create authentic, immersive environments with** visual related live themes and decoration. The scene is the first visual perception of the user when they see lives. It can be based on the production scene, transaction scene, marketing scene, usage scene, etc.

   

   Scene innovation is an effective tool to attract users to enter the live room and continue watching by **solving user trust issues and triggering emotional resonance.** For sellers, distinctive scenes can better showcase the features and value of products, which will boost live data uplift.

   

   

   #### **Detail standards:**

| Innovation Elements | Innovation Type | Description | Innovation Sub-type | Detail Requirements / Standards | Good Cases (2-3 Cases) | Bad Cases and Why (2-3 Cases) |

# **FAQ**

1. **Why is the number of "In queue" videos decreasing so slowly?**

The exploration speed is related to campaign spending. Since only a small portion of the budget is allocated to new creative exploration, if the overall spending is low, the exploration speed will be slow. We recommend using Creative Boost to speed up the exploration of high-potential videos, or using Max Delivery to boost product cold starts and generate initial spending for the campaign.

2. **Why are most videos in "Not delivering"? Creative utilization rate is too low\!**

Since GMV Max automatically retrieves all available videos, including those posted a year ago, the video pool is very large, resulting in a low creative utilization rate. However, most of these videos are low-quality and outdated. If clients continue to use GMV Max, and not all new videos become "Delivering" videos, the creative utilization rate will decrease further. Therefore, this metric is not meaningful. Clients should focus on gaining insights from "Delivering" videos and use Creative Boost to test high-potential "Not Delivering" videos.
