Douyin EC Conversion Channel

• Live Room: An order placed directly from a EC live room, regardless of the traffic source (e.g.,
recommendation, following page, shop tab, search, or a short video that led to the live room)

• Short Video: An order placed directly from a EC short video, regardless of the traffic source (eg,

recommendation, following page, shop tab, search)

• Image/Text: An order placed directly from a EC image/text post, regardless of the traffic source (eg,

recommendation, following page, shop tab, search)

• Prod Card: An order placed directly from a EC product card, regardless of the traffic source (eg,

recommendation, following page, shop tab, search)
