## DP Development Stages

### Stage 1 – Exploration (2019–2020)
**Capability Evolution**: Survival depended on who could first build a closed loop for "content-driven commerce." Core competencies concentrated on short-video creative production and KOL matchmaking.

**Market Characteristics**: Douyin E-commerce in early "short-video product link" stage; livestreaming just emerged. Rules imperfect, traffic costs extremely low.

**Positive Case**: A Hangzhou MCN – leveraged short-video expertise for beauty brand, one video drove 500k+ sales without large ads, pivoted to professional provider.

**Negative Case**: Amateur providers with no merchandising/after-sales knowledge caused high return rates (80%) and store rating drops.

### Stage 2 – Expansion (2021-2023)
**Market characteristics**: Interest E-commerce upgraded into full-funnel; GMV grew exponentially; Number of DPs surged from hundreds to thousands.

**Capability evolution**: Live operations, paid ads and store live systems became basic skills. Key divider: ability to help brands build always-on store live capability.

**Positive Case**: A Beijing DP helped home appliance brand build short-video seeding + brand self-live + Creator matrix, Double 11 store live single-day GMV to RMB 20M.

**Negative Case**: DP signing 30+ SMEs with low-price promises and recycled content led to under-delivery and churn.

**Key conclusion**: Agencies must move from single-point breakthrough to systematic full-funnel operations.

### Stage 3 – Divergence (2024-2025)
**Market characteristics: "Ice vs. Fire"**: Some Sellers grow rapidly; others stall. Platform algorithm shifts from "transaction-first" to "watch time-first", stricter health metrics.

**Capability evolution**: Full-Funnel Upgrade – data-driven decisioning + content quality + full-funnel coordination.

**Positive Case**: A Hangzhou DP with own data platform and AIGC content engine grew beauty brand monthly GMV from RMB 5M to RMB 18M.

**Negative Case**: Low-priced white-label partner with templated content faced traffic restrictions, lost 60% of clients.

### Stage 4 – Intelligent Refined Operations (2026- Now)
**Market characteristics**: Full-funnel integration + AI-driven precision operations. New products like Qianchuan · Chengfang offer AI-powered smart marketing.

**Capability evolution**: Master deep AI tooling, cross-platform data integration, and omni-channel strategy.

**Positive Case**: A Shenzhen DP fully deployed AI by late 2025 – AIGC generates hundreds of unique daily videos, CAC dropped 22%, GMV grew 35% YoY.

**Negative Case**: Partners rejecting AI lagged in efficiency and lost clients.

## Six Common Pricing Models

| Model | Format | Best For | Pros & Cons |
|-------|--------|----------|-------------|
| Fixed Retainer | Fixed monthly/annual fee, not tied to GMV | Basic management services | Pro: Stable income. Con: Lacks performance incentive. |
| CPS Commission | Percentage of net sales (typically 8%-15%) | Live streaming, Creator affiliate | Pro: Strongly tied to results, high incentive. |
| Hybrid (Retainer + Comm.) | Base fee + sales commission + tiered bonuses | Comprehensive management | Pro: Guarantees base income + performance upside. |
| Ad Fee + Rebate | Fee based on a percentage of ad spend | Qianchuan ad services | Pro: Pay-for-performance, manageable risk. |
| Project-Based Fee | One-time quote for a specific project | One-off campaigns, cold starts | Pro: Flexible. Con: Lacks recurring revenue. |
| Guarantee + Over-achievement | Guarantees base GMV, high split on excess | Deep brand partnerships | Pro: Shared risk and reward. |
